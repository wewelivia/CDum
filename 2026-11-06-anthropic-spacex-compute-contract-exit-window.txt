Anthropic has signed up for close to $900bn of compute, and most of it cannot be cancelled. The exception is the $120.5bn it has agreed with SpaceX. Once the initial terms end in early 2027, either side can walk away on 90 days’ notice. That makes it the only large contract in Anthropic’s book that can move within a quarter. If compute stays scarce, SpaceX can take the capacity back for its own models. If AI demand falls short, Anthropic can hand it back. Either move would tell us something the locked contracts cannot, because by design they don’t move. SpaceX has more riding on this than Anthropic does. Anthropic reportedly made up a third of SpaceX’s revenue last quarter, and at the full contracted rate it would be closer to half. The earliest exits land in the second quarter of 2027. That is the window we are watching.
