Welcome to our “Outlook 2027”, the investment strategy update from Barclays Private Bank and Wealth Management.

2026 kept investors on their toes. An energy shock revived inflation and led the Federal Reserve to raise rates for the first time in three years. Long-term borrowing costs climbed to their highest in more than two decades. Yet equities reached new records, carried by the extraordinary earnings of companies at the heart of artificial intelligence (AI).

Our message for 2027 is simple: stay invested until the music stops. The tune is one of strong earnings and resilient growth, both closely tied to AI. It can keep playing for some time, but its tempo will depend on two prices: the price of energy and the price of money.

In the following chapters, we look at how these forces play out across the largest economies and what they mean for bonds and equities. Higher yields have made the backdrop more supportive for income investors than it has been in years, even if fiscal strains argue for caution on duration. In equities, we expect earnings rather than valuations to set the pace, with leadership likely to keep rotating.

No one can know when the music will stop. That is why we spend as much time on how portfolios are built as on what we expect. When one theme runs through so much of the market, diversifying by what drives returns, rather than by asset class labels, matters more than ever.
